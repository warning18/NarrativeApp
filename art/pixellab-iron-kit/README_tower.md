# Tower decorations (the iron kit's Tower environment set)
Three sets for the Tower floors' bands, in the exact format of the other environments (`env/<name>_{top,bottom,corner}_<0..2>.png`: top 32x10, bottom 32x8, corner 16x16 thin L-piece, transparent, own 1 px ink; anchors in `anchors_tower.json`): **`tower_night`** (hanging chains of open links, candle wax drips, candle stubs with a flame at the bottom, a chain-draped corner), **`tower_stone`** (cracks running from the edge, dust, rubble chips, a cracked corner), **`tower_void`** (violet cracks, a little void glow, wisps rising, a cracked corner with two light-violet glints). They sit on every material tier (iron, silver, gilt shown on `docs/art/boards/tower-decorations.png`, light and dark, 3x, with the HUD panel and a button, corners only on buttons). Separate sprites, never baked into a frame. Generator `tower_gen.py` (repo root). 0 generations. Not wired; UX-2 maps a Tower floor band (night, stone, void) to a set.
