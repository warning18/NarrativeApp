Reads best at 48; the thin callings (bow, daggers) are fine at 32 and small at 16.
- **B, non-square containers:** each race on a heater **shield** in its colour (red, teal, grey, green, violet) with the emblem in the middle; each calling on a **hanging banner** (a wooden bar and a notched pennant in rust, blue, brown, charcoal, gold). One family, readable at 16.
Recommendation: **B for lists (16 px)** where a colour block helps scanning, **A at 48** where the emblem can stand alone; or one option throughout: the user's call. Not wired.
