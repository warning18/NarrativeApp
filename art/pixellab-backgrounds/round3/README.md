Staged `staged/<plate>_s11|s12.png` (180x320, palette PNG8, 24 colours), raw downloads in `raw/`, generator `../round3_gen.py` (prompts inside). **30 generations** (balance 1113.5 to 1083.5). No figures, no text. Sheets: `docs/art/boards/backgrounds-round3-tower.png` and `-fight.png` (real size), and `-2x.png` of each. Nothing wired; the user picks one seed a plate.
My suggestions (not decisions): **Tower** boss10 s11 (the warm Inquisition hall) or s12; boss20 s12 (reads as a room); boss30 s12 (violet fissures on a black tower); ward5 s11; ward15 s11 (the bell); ward25 s12 (the glass chapel); win s12 (dawn on the parapet); lose s12 (the cell with the brazier). **Fight** fields s12, ford s12, burnt s11, cliffs s12, terraces s12, reeds s12, hall s11.
Honest notes: the Tower prompts asked for interiors; several seeds show the tower from outside (boss20 s11, boss30, ward5 s12, win, lose s11): they read well as banners for the floor but are not rooms, so the two or three interior plates (boss10, ward15, ward25, boss20 s12) are the safest for fights. The fight stages keep the lower half empty for the fighters; as before, open scenes would keep the sky in code from the horizon down (fields, cliffs, burnt) and the interior ones (hall, reeds) are full-bleed.
