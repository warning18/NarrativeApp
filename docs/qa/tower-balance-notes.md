**The totals sit inside Core's bands** (median 13 to 18, floor 20 at 25 to 38%, top 5 to 12% with no unlocks, 15 to 25% with all). Two ranges of 2,000 seeds, both ways (1 to 2000 and 10001 to 12000): median floor 17 and 17, floor 20 at 34.3% and 34.7%, top with no unlocks 10.2% and 10.2%, with all unlocks 16.4% and 17.2% (the low end of its band). The guards of `test/qa_tower_sim_test.dart` hold the bands on seeds 1 to 500.

1. **The clans are now level**: with no clan at the start, the "clan" of a run is its first oath. Dominion wins the top 10.4% (floor 20 at 37.0%), Penitents 10.9%, Mire 9.2%: a spread of 1.7 points, against 4.7% to 15.0% in v1.848. With all unlocks the six clans win 14.5% (Compact) to 17.7% (Dominion).
2. **The professions moved again**: the mage now wins the top 13.5% (9.2% warrior, 7.8% rogue), against 8.3% in v1.848; the rogue still reaches floor 20 most (41.9% against 31.2% for the mage and 30.2% for the warrior) but wins the top least. Spread of the top rate 7.8% to 13.5%, wider than v1.848's 8.3% to 11.5%. With all unlocks: cleric 16.2%, ranger 16.3%, mage 18.8%, rogue 14.0%, warrior 17.1%.
3. **Never sworn: 1 run in 2,000** (it died on floor 1). The bot takes an oath at once when it is offered, so the bot cannot say what a player who refuses every oath does; the table "clans sworn" shows only that runs that swore to one or two clans died early (median floor 5 and 8: they died before the oaths came), and that the 3-clan runs, with no unlocks, are 88% of all runs and win 11.5%.
4. **The bosses**: win rate of the runs that reach the floor, no unlocks: floor 5 warden 98.0%, 10 boss 95.8%, 15 warden 89.3%, 20 boss 84.8%, 25 warden 80.8%, 30 top boss 91.0%. The floor 20 boss is the hardest (84.8%; 80.0% with all unlocks, lower than with none, as in v1.848) and the floor 25 warden next; the top boss is easier than the two before it. Runs end most on ordinary floors, as before.
5. The bot never refuses a card, so a run earns 26.3 marks on average (median 25; 30.9 with all unlocks).

**What the bot cannot say (Core's note).** It plays on the headless fight (`simulateSimFight`): that fight has **no affixes** (the real one has them, so the real floors are harder), and the clan die's resonance and the specialisations are not simulated. The splits above are findings to check, not verdicts.
