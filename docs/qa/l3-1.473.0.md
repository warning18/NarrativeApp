## Simulator, compared with 1.285.0

Same method as before (`SIM_GOALS=random,favorGood,favorEvil,maximizeGold`,
seed 7 for the 100 runs, seeds 11 and 23 for the 800), so the shape compares, but
**the level does not**: three rules changed on purpose since 1.285.0, the
difficulty review (v1.429: every profession wins about 70% of its fights), the
smaller scale (v1.458: fight numbers divided by 5, one pick a level) and the
simulator's own turn-in politics (v1.464).

| measure | 1.285.0 | **1.473.0** |
|---|---|---|
| attempt win, 800 runs | 45.0% | **36.1%** |
| encounter win, 800 runs (an encounter is lost after 3 failed attempts) | not reported | **69.2%** |
| encounter win, 100 runs | not reported | 71.3% |
| level and gold at the end (800 runs) | 13.1, 4,286 (100 runs) | 21.0, 6,019 |
| per profession, attempt win (warrior / rogue / ranger / cleric / mage) | 30 / 42 / 40 / 62 / 56% | 35.9 / 33.6 / 41.1 / 34.1 / 35.8% |
| per profession, encounter win (same order) | not reported | 69.4 / 66.7 / 74.6 / 66.2 / 68.7% |

- **The class gap is closed.** At 1.285.0 the warrior's attempt win was 30%
  against the cleric's 62%; it is now 35.9% against 34.1%, and the encounter win
  sits between 66% and 75% for all five professions, which is what v1.429 aimed
  at. That observation of the last L3 is
  resolved.
- **By chapter** (encounter win, 800 runs): 94, 90, 69, 59, 68, 61, 64, 53% for
  chapters 1 to 8. The dip is chapters 3 to 4 and 8.
- **The bosses** (encounters won, 800 runs): Aurel Vane 79, void stalker 71,
  Hollow Court inquisitor 65, bone warden 67, void archon 70, white admiral 49,
  claimants Vane 52, Tallis 64 and Morrow 53, tear herald 39, void sovereign 44.
  Mean **59.4%**, against 47.9% at 1.285.0 and the boss curve's targets (75, 65,
  55, 55, 50, 45, 40, 40, 40, 35). Every boss is at or above its target, the void
  archon (+20) and claimant Tallis (+24) the furthest. Observation for Core: the
  later bosses are easier than the curve says since the scale and difficulty
  changes.
- **The enemies lost most** (encounter loss, 800 runs): Brother Clement 66%,
  inquisition soldier 65%, inquisition auxiliary 62%, tear herald 61%, void
  hound 59%, knell keeper 58%. The soldiers and the auxiliaries are packs, which
  is why a plain enemy tops a boss; worth a look beside the 70% aim, not a
  defect.
- **Each run's endings:** all 900 runs end at "Begin again, changed" (no step
  cap, no broken link, no dead end).

## Finding (for UX)

**On a small phone the story text gets very little room when a scene has
choices.** In the story player at 320 x 568 (the smallest phone
this app is checked on), a scene with three choices (scene 800, "Captured,
then. I woke in the Black Hold...") shows **one line of the text** between the
status row and the choices; the walking companion's strip and the choices take
the rest (`story_800_320_en.png`). At 360 x 740 it is four or five lines
(`story_800_360_fr.png`, `story_6002_360_en.png`). The text scrolls inside that
window (and the status row and the companion collapse as the player reads down
the page), and `story_player_screen.dart` reserves "a few lines of the story"
(72 px) on purpose, so this is a design choice and not an overflow. With the
typewriter on, the choices wait for the text, so what a player sees while it
writes itself out is not covered here. If it matters: the window could keep a
share of the height (a third of the area) when the text is long, or the choices
could fold to their first line until the text is read to the end. The
epilogue (scene 7005) shows the same way: its title, then the "The End" card,
the narration scrolling in a one-line window.
