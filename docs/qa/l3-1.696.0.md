## Simulator (same method as 1.489 and 1.501)

`SIM_GOALS=random,favorGood,favorEvil,maximizeGold`, seed 7 for 100 runs, seeds 11 and 23 for the 800 (400 each), `test/sim_playthroughs_test.dart`; the coverage test on 300 runs, seed 7; the clan-power study on 50 runs. The figures of 1.501.0 are reproduced to the digit by [`l3_sim_metrics.py`](l3_sim_metrics.py) from that run's data (attempt win 34.4, encounter win 66.8, level 20.8, gold 4,570), so the comparison is like for like.

**Read it as a shape, not a regression test: the simulator and the game changed under the numbers.** Since 1.501 the simulated hero walks in the full world (the specialisations, Vica from the first chapter, the ports and detour scenes: [sim-world.md](sim-world.md)), the simulator uses the fight's own damage sum (v1.653), and the game gained the Annex's +40% companion health (v1.695), the clans' dice and the fighters' cap.

| per chapter, encounter win (1 to 8) | 94, 87, 67, 57, 67, 58, 54, 49 | **94, 95, 75, 68, 75, 71, 59, 61** |

- **The bosses are on the curve.** Encounter win against the curve's target, 800 runs: Aurel Vane 72 (75), void stalker 61 (65), Hollow Court inquisitor 55 (55), bone warden 56 (55), void archon 50 (50), white admiral 51 (45), claimants Vane 35, Tallis 42, Morrow 45 (40), tear herald 37 (35), void sovereign 32 (35). **Mean 48.7%** (48.4 at 1.501), every boss within 6 points of its target; the three bosses that sat 5 to 10 points under (the Void Sovereign, Morrow, the tear herald) are now within 3, 5 and 2. The re-sizing of v1.613 and v1.614 did its job.
- **But the ordinary fights are easier: encounter win 75.5% against 66.8%** (+8.7 points), in every chapter (+0 to +13: chapter 2 95, chapter 4 68, chapter 6 71, chapter 8 61). The 1.473 difficulty review aimed at 70%. The bosses did not move, so the extra ease is in the regular enemies. **For Core and the user:** is 75% the intended feel, or should the regular fights be pulled back to about 70?
- **The mage leads the other professions by 5 to 8 points** (80.6% against 72.7 to 75.4; the spread 7.9, was 1.9 at 1.501). `boss_balance_test` holds the spread at 8 or less, so it passes, but it is at the edge: the next change that helps the mage fails it. **For Core.** (The 100-run seed 7 batch is too small for this: 20 runs a profession.)
- **The enemies lost most** (800 runs): the Void Sovereign 68% (75 at 1.501), claimant Vane 65 (n=101), the tear herald 63 (68), claimant Tallis 58, the strand colossus 58, Brother Clement 58 (64).
- **Coverage** (300 runs, seed 7): the story now has **479 scenes** (440), 1,156 ways (1,061), 51 quests (49); exploring runs reach **443 (92.5%)**, random runs 421 (87.9%); at 1.501 it was 426 of 440 (96.8%) and 405. Unvisited by any run: scene 270 (the Weaver's Alley, parked and unreachable by design) and **21 scenes added since 1.501 that no simulated run takes: 8 companion loyalty errands, 6 "names" and 6 "price" camp errands of the throne, and `7200_will_choice`**, plus the 13 later-variant scenes already unvisited at 1.501. They are camp errands (`campErrand`, entered from the cove camp) the simulator does not take; the graph guard, `qa_story_blocking_test`, treats them as enterable. **For Core, low:** the simulator could take them.
- **Clan power** (50 runs, mean at the start of chapter 8, against 1.501): the Dominion **56** (73), the Compact 58 (58), the Vigil 44 (41), the Penitents **40** (25), the Mire **27** (10), the Crows 46 (48). The Dominion no longer runs away with the coast (the rival attacks of wave 18), and the Mire and the Penitents no longer collapse. The shape the 1.501 report asked for.
