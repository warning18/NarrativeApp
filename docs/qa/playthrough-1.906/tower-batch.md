# The Tower: balance report (QA, phase 2)

Core's bot (`playTowerRun`, `lib/data/tower/tower_bot.dart`) over 2,000 seeds (1 to 2000), once with no unlocks and once with all of them, on the engine of v1.855 (spoils and boons, #854). The bot is naive by design: it takes the clan's die, better gear, a die, a skill, a spell, the clan's stat, then a heal (a heal first below half health); it never refuses a card. Generated by `tool/qa_tower_report.py` from `test/qa_tower_sim_test.dart`; the guards in that test hold the targets below on seeds 1 to 500 on every pull request.
