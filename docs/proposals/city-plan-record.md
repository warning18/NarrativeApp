# The city plan record (board 15), Core → UX

The data UX paints the city plan from (model A, top-down, organic). Core
generates it and stores it; UX paints it, zooms it, fogs it; Narrative
names the buildings. First city: Saltmouth.

## Where it lives

- `assets/gamedata/city_plans.json`, one record per city, keyed by the
  city's geography id (`saltmouth`).
- Read through `lib/data/city_plan_record.dart`: `CityPlanRecord` (the
  geometry, typed: `List<Offset>` for every polygon or path) and
  `cityPlanRecordsProvider` (all records, by city id).
- Generated by `lib/data/city_plan_generator.dart` from a seed (a Voronoi
  plan with lots, the shore, a river with an island, canals, an avenue
  star, a ring for the arena). The stored record is what the game reads; the
  generator only makes a new one.

## Units

The plan's own square, **1000 x 1000**, origin top left, y down (the same
as the old `CityPlan`, so `PlacePlan` still fits). Every point is
`[x, y]` in whole units; every polygon is a closed ring given once (the
last point is not repeated). The painter scales the square to the screen.

## The record

```json
"saltmouth": {
  "seed": 1203,
  "sea":     [[x, y], ...],                 // the water beyond the shore, a polygon; [] inland
  "river":   {"width": 28, "path": [[x, y], ...]},   // null if none
  "islands": [[[x, y], ...]],               // polygons in the river or the sea
  "canals":  [{"width": 10, "path": [[x, y], ...]}],
  "wall":    {"ring": [[x, y], ...],
              "gates":  [{"id": "gate_landward", "at": [x, y]}],
              "towers": [[x, y], ...]},
  "avenues": [{"width": 16, "path": [[x, y], ...]}],   // the star from the gates and the main square
  "streets": [{"width": 7,  "path": [[x, y], ...]}],   // the lanes between the lots
  "bridges": [{"at": [x, y], "angle": 35, "length": 40}],  // angle in degrees, along the bridge
  "squares": [{"id": "sq_market", "kind": "market", "shape": [[x, y], ...]}],
                                            // kind: market | arena | plaza | quay
  "districts": {
    "saltmouth_wharf": {"shape": [[x, y], ...], "label": [x, y], "entry": [x, y]}
  },
  "buildings": [{"id": "saltmouth_b012", "district": "saltmouth_wharf",
                 "kind": "house", "shape": [[x, y], ...]}],
                                            // kind: house | hall | temple | warehouse | tower | stall | shed
  "anchors": {"2016": "saltmouth_b012"},    // a way (its target scene id) → the building its pin stands on
  "names":   {"saltmouth_b012": {"en": "The Net-Loft", "fr": "Le Grenier aux filets"}}
}
```

- **districts**: every district of the city in geography.json, as a
  polygon that tiles the walled town (plus the shore and the yard outside
  the wall where they lie). `label` is where its name goes; `entry` is where
  the party stands when it walks in (on an avenue or a square).
- **anchors**: one per way. A way is a choice of a scene in the city; it
  stands in the district of the scene it leads to (that scene's
  `location`). `CityPlanRecord.anchorOf(targetId, districtId)` gives the
  building: the record's own anchor when it lies in that district, else the
  next free building of that district (a hall or temple first, the nearest to
  the district's label), the same every time. So when Narrative re-tags a scene
  into another district, its pin moves there without a new record.
- **names**: Narrative's (English and French), for buildings that carry a way
  or a landmark; a building without a name shows its way's own name.

## What Core gives the screens besides the record

- `waysInDistrict(...)`: the hub's ways whose scene lies in a given district
  ("show the party's district's ways").
- `partyDistrictProvider`: the district the party stands in, in its city;
  it follows the scene the story is at, and **free walking** sets it to any
  district the party has found (no story choice, no time, no road).
- `foundDistricts(...)`: the districts the party has found (a scene played
  there, or walked into), for the fog; the rest are fogged.
- geography.json: two new districts of Saltmouth, **The Lantern Alleys**
  (`saltmouth_lantern_alleys`) and **Card Row** (`saltmouth_card_row`).
  Their names and blurbs are placeholders until Narrative words them.

## Open to UX

The record can grow fields the painter needs (trees, fields outside the wall,
roof colours by kind). Ask through the manager; Core adds them to the
generator and the record.
