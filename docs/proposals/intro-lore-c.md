## 1. The burning banner

Nothing in the data calls the Shroud a clan banner, or says it flew over Alster. What the data says:
- the Shroud is one cloth the **first Inquisitor cut into six pieces** (heirloom, moon shard, Warden's standard, reliquary thread, White Fleet sail, Sovereign's mantle);
- the first piece is the cloth in oilcloth that lay **under the player's family house** in Alster (the "Grey Shroud" to the salt-cutters, the "Void Banner" to the Inquisition);
- scene 0 says power is *worn, not held*; the Inquisition sews its saints into **white standards**.

So a whole banner burning *on the night* would contradict the lore: it was cut long before. Two readings fit, pick one for UX:
- **A (recommended): a vision of before.** The whole grey banner burns and vanishes as an image, a legend, not an event of the night. It comes apart into **six embers**; one falls toward Alster (the piece under the house). The fire is the Inquisition's, the city's burning, not a clan's act. The six pieces survive as embers, which also tells the player there are six.
- **B: the Inquisition's white standard** burning over the city. Safe, but it is not the Shroud and loses the point.

## 2. The tavern

**The Blind Beggar**, Tallow Street, Lower Town of Alster. Geography id `blind_beggar` (building, "Le Mendiant Aveugle"); city plan building `alster_b000`; its floor plan is the room of scenes 100, 105, 110, 120. **Yes, the first playable scene (100) happens there.** Scene 100 even has the beat: the player rolls the old bone die once for luck, it comes up **six**, and then the wall comes in. So the die should stop on a six.

## 3. The bombing

The **Inquisition's white ships**, flying low over the roofs ("they were white, and they were in the air, and their sails were painted with signs that burned like lamps", scene 250). They fire iron balls ("a ball from one of the white ships", scene 400). Not the Dominion's engines, not the clans. Landmarks for the skyline: the **harbor's old stone giant** standing out of the smoke, fire in its lap; doors chalked with a white sign; masked inquisitors with lanterns and chalk going door to door.
