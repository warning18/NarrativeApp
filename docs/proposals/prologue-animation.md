# The opening scene as an animation: three storyboards (UX-2, pictures only)

The user wants the wall of text of the first scene (`docs/feedback/prologue-text-screen.jpg`)
replaced by an animation where the text arrives directly and flows into the character choice.
Pictures: `docs/ux-shots/prologue-animation/storyboard-A.png`, `-B.png`, `-C.png` (four frames each,
phone width, French). Narrative's words and the bold phrases (un vieux dé d'os; le pouvoir ne se
tient pas ; il se porte) are unchanged; no shorter lines are needed.

Common to all: about 28 s if not skipped. A tap finishes the sentence being shown; a second tap
jumps to the end. The Peuple / Vocation choice is on screen and usable at the end, no extra tap.
Reduced motion: all the text at once, still, and the choice appears with a simple fade.
"Revenir à la carte" stays as a quiet link at the bottom.

**A, "La voix".** One large sentence at a time, arriving word by word; earlier sentences rise and
dim. The bold phrases glow gold as they arrive. At the end the text folds into a strip at the top
(tap to reread) and the choice rises beneath it. The most sober.

**B, "Le dé d'os".** The text arrives word by word at the foot of the screen. At "un vieux dé d'os"
a die rolls across and lands; it stays lit under the last sentence, then splits into the two
cards, Peuple and Vocation. The most identity; more tuning (the die's path, sound if wanted).

**C, "La lueur".** The whole text is already there, in shadow as today; a candle light moves down
and reveals it line by line (read lines dim, bold phrases turn gold when lit), then widens into
dawn on the choice. The least animated; keeps free reading.
