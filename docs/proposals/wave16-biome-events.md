# Wave 16, item 3: biome events for frost, fen and sea cliffs (Narrative, sketch, EN only)

For `docs/proposals/wave16-core.md` §3. The desert has 6 events on its crash route (sandstorm, dry well, spring, mirage, scavengers, shade) and the ashlands 7 more (flash flood, water seller, ash squall, Inquisition patrol, burned shrine, feral dogs, falling wall). Each new route (frost, fen, sea cliffs) gets **3 to 4 events of its own**, the same shape: a short scene, 2 or 3 choices, one of them a check (an ability and a DC), the other a way round that costs a day or a little health, and a `note` for each outcome. `healthPct`, `gold`, `days` and the numbers are Core's; the weights are a route's `events` map (the plain ones count twice, as the sandstorm does).

| Biome | Chapter | Event | The scene | The choices |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Frost** | 4 to 5 (the road to the Reliquary) | **The pilgrim's cairn** | A cairn of coins and mittens on the verge, each stone with a name scratched into it. | Leave a coin (free, small luck); take the warm mittens (Dexterity, nothing watches; or the frost keeps your hands). |
| | | **The rime fall** | The trees let go of their frost all at once, a white hour. | Push through (Constitution); wait it out in the lee of a rock (a day). |
| | | **The hound track** | Paw-prints made of frost, going the same way as you, and a day old. | Follow and find what made them (Wisdom, a fight or a find); change road (a day). |
| | | **The bell on the road** | A bell that rings by itself on a post, counting something. | Listen until you hear what it counts (Wisdom, a clue or a chill); walk past with your hands over your ears (free, a little health). |
| **Fen** | 5 | **The reed guide's lantern** | A lantern hung on a reed at the water's edge, with no one near. | Follow it (Wisdom: it is a guide, or it is bait); keep to your own path (a day). |
| | | **The sucking mud** | A dry-looking crossing that is not. | Cross at a run (Dexterity); lay boughs and cross slow (a day, no cost); a companion pulls you out (free, if one walks with you). |
| | | **The drowned cart** | A wreck of a pilgrim cart with a box still lashed to it. | Dive for the box (Strength, a find); cut the lashings from the bank (Dexterity, less to gain); leave it (free). |
| | | **The returned child** | A child standing in the reeds who knows your name, and is, quite possibly, not a child. | Speak kindly (Charisma, a blessing or a lie); give her bread and move on (free, small); turn your back (health, the fen remembers). |
| **Sea cliffs** | 4 to 7 | **The wrecker's lamp** | A false light on the cliff top, set to bring a ship in. | Put it out (Dexterity, a good deed with a price); take the wreckers' cut (free, gold and a debt); warn the ship (Charisma, standing with the sea clans). |
| | | **The salt wind** | A gale off the water that makes the path a ledge. | Edge along it (Dexterity); go inland a day (a day lost, no risk). |
| | | **The gull colony** | A cliff face white with nests, and eggs for the taking. | Climb for eggs (Strength, food and health); leave them (free); throw a stone to scatter them (a hazard behind you). |
| | | **The tide stair** | A stair cut in the rock that the tide covers twice a day. | Run it before the tide (Constitution); wait for the ebb (a day). |

## Notes
- Chapter 7's route stays mild: only the cliff events with a free way round (the salt wind, the tide stair), and none that costs more than a few percent of health.
- Each event can carry a `ruler` key later (the six ruler events of `wave16-ruler-events.md` are a separate pool, drawn 1 in 3); the biome events are the other 2 in 3.
- The fen events lean on the Mire's clan lore (Heron's Wake, the Returned); the frost events on the Reliquary Quarter's; the cliff events on the Crows and the White Anchorage's.
- All of it is open to the user's change; replacing a premise changes nothing in the plan. Texts (EN and FR) follow once the user picks the item.
