# Glass Shore crossing events (Narrative, for wave 17 Core item 4)

Four events in `assets/gamedata/land_events.json`, EN and FR, shaped like the frost and fen ones. Plain biome events, no `ruler` or `band`. Core's route uses them as its glass-shore pool; none is drawn yet.

| id | what it is | check / way round | worst fail |
|---|---|---|---|
| `glass_fulgurite` | the dune fused into black glass | dexterity 12 / go round by the wet sand | -7% |
| `glass_hull_shelter` | a White Fleet hull in the dunes, a wind rising | wisdom 12 / sit in its lee | -5% |
| `glass_drowned_well` | a well from a drowned coast, fresh water | constitution 11 / leave a coin | -6% |
| `glass_tear_pull` | the tear calls across the water | wisdom 13 or charisma 11 | -8% |

Premises come from the Glass Shore's own blurbs (grey sand drawn from the dead heart, the White Fleet's wrecks behind the dunes, a village from a drowned coast, the tear standing open). Each has a free or near-free way round except `glass_tear_pull`, which offers two checks. Numbers are drafts: Core sets `dc` and `healthPct` against chapter 6, whose encounters are won 56.8% of the time.
