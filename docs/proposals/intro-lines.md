# Intro lines (draft)

Status: **draft for the user to pick a tone.** No build, no version.

For UX's 10–15 s intro before the race and profession choice. Four lines per set, one every 3–4 s, each under 14 words (the longest is 13). Each set ends on the invitation to choose. The player in chapter 1 is a local ("our house" on Tallow Street), so the lines never call them a stranger or a newcomer; it is the choice of *who* that is open. Gender-neutral; French uses « vous ».

Facts used, all from chapter 1: Alster at dusk, the Lower Town and Tallow Street, the Inquisition, the clans, the harbor's stone giant (scene 250), the night the white ships come (the Blind Beggar scene, 100, is that evening). The Inquisition's bell is new to the intro: chapter 1 only rings the Lark's bell (890s), so keep it a bell in the storyboard, not a named tower. Ash is from the burning that follows, a little early on purpose.

## Notes for the storyboard
- Line 1 sits on the city at dusk (ash), line 2 on the bell, line 3 on the clans, line 4 holds the screen on the choice: the player picks next.
- If the intro must be shorter, lines 1 and 4 stand alone in every set.
- The lines are not translated word for word; the French keeps the tone of each set.
