# Proposal: painted parallax layers for the road-crossing scene (text; 0 generations; the desert pilot comes after the user's pick)
**What it is today** (read in `lib/widgets/side_view/`): a scene is a stack of code-drawn layers on a **72-row grid** (one scene pixel = box height / 72: about 2.64 logical px at the default 190 px height; about 156 scene pixels wide at 412, 136 at 360), painted back to front and scrolled at their own share of the walk: sky 0 (dithered bands), sun 0.05, far mesas with the goal's silhouette 0.15, far dunes 0.15, mid dunes 0.4, near dunes with props 0.8, path 1.0, then the actors (the ronin's feet at row 66, x 96; speed 0), the hour wash of `kit_hour.dart`, and the weather particles. Six kits: desert, ruins, frost, fen, cliffs, sea. Every layer is a sine hill with a rim: flat shapes beside the painted fight stages and story bands.
**What I would paint** (per kit, as layers the engine already scrolls; the engine only needs a layer that draws an image tiled, UX-2's part):
| layer | size (scene pixels) | opaque? | tiles left-right? | scroll |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| sky (the dithered bands and cloud/haze, the sun disc stays code) | 160 x 52, rows 0 to 51 | opaque | no (static) | 0 |
| far (mesas / far hills / far ruins; the goal silhouette stays code) | 192 x 24, rows about 28 to 52 | transparent above its skyline | **yes, seamless** | 0.15 |
| mid | 192 x 20, rows about 38 to 58 | transparent above | yes | 0.4 |
| near (dunes plus the props: rocks, dry brush) | 192 x 24, rows about 48 to 72 | transparent above | yes | 0.8 |
| ground / path | stays code | | | 1.0 |
**Stays code:** the hour wash and stars (`kit_hour.dart`: dawn, dusk, night tint laid over everything, so painted layers take it automatically), the sandstorm, snow, rain, fog and shimmer particles, the path the figures walk on (so each kit's path colour and the encounter figures stay consistent), the sun disc, the goal silhouette (driven by progress), the ronin and the party, the encounter figures. **Why:** they are what changes with game state; the layers are the unchanging landscape.
**How I get tileable layers out of PixelLab:** (1) each layer is generated on its own as a transparent strip (`create_image_pixen`, `no_background`, 1 generation), with the **fight plate of the same place as the forced palette** (`color_image`) so the crossing and the fight share colours (a place looks the same in its fight stage and its scenes: desert with `dunes`, frost with `snow`, fen with `reeds`, cliffs with `cliffs`, ruins with `ruins`, sea with `waves`); (2) the seam: I shift the strip by half its width so the seam is in the middle and repair it with `inpaint_image` (1 generation) or by hand (blend the two edges over 12 columns, 0 generations), then shift back; a check composes the layer twice side by side. Mirroring a half-width strip is the free fallback (a visible symmetry, not wanted for the far mesas). (3) The sky is opaque and static: 1 generation, no seam.
**Cost per kit:** sky 1 + far 1 + mid 1 + near 1 = 4 first tries, up to 4 seam repairs (0 if done by hand) and 2 retries = **about 6 to 10 a kit**; all six kits **about 36 to 60** (above 30: your OK needed before the batch). The sea kit is different (water bands, a hull, no ground): same count, a different set of layers (sky, far sea, mid swell, near foam).
**Order I would take:** the desert as a pilot (the user's reference kit), 8 generations at most; if liked, frost and fen next (the two the player meets most), then cliffs, ruins, sea.
**Risks, honestly:** a generated strip may not tile well (hence the seam step); the horizon rows of the three layers must keep their order (far above mid above near) so the depth reads: I check each against the 72-row grid; the layers' colours follow the fight plate, so the hour wash does the rest.
**Needs from UX-2:** an image layer in the engine (draw an `ui.Image` tiled at `scroll / unit`), the real box size on a phone, and a switch back to the code kit while a place has no painted layers.

## Confirmed by UX-2 (read in `land_crossing_screen.dart` and `side_view.dart`)
- The box is the full screen width (360 or 412 px) by a fixed 190 px (`_crossingSceneHeight`): unit 190/72 = 2.64, about 136 scene pixels wide at 360 and 156 at 412; a tall phone changes nothing. The HUD bar sits over the top of the box: **keep the top 12 rows free of detail that must read.**
- A `SideLayer` is `paint(canvas, size, scroll, t, unit)`, so an image layer is a `drawImageRect` with `FilterQuality.none`, tiled at `(scroll / unit) mod tileWidth`, two draws to wrap; a tile must join to itself at its two edges. The strips load ahead and the code kit paints until they are ready (nothing waits on art). Far, mid, near only cover their own rows (transparent elsewhere); the sky is opaque.
- The sun and the goal silhouette stay code (the goal moves to a small code layer over the far image); the actors, path and weather stay code. The draw order is layers (with the hour's stars), actors, the hour wash over all, particles: a painted layer is tinted by the wash like a code one. Dusk and night are a tint over the day picture, not a repaint; a **night set of tiles** per layer is possible, switched by phase, but is more art (about +4 generations a kit).
- Caution: the same kits also dress the fight ground and the life-choices header (`origin_scene_backdrop`), so a painted desert would show there too (consistent). Say if the pilot is crossing-only.
