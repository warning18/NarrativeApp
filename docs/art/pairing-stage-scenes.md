# Pairing: fight stage against story places, and the consistency check (text; 0 generations)
The user's rule: **a place looks the same in its fight stage and in its story scenes** (same architecture and materials, palette and accent, hour and weather, landmark props). This page pairs each `FightStage` with the story locations whose fights use it, says whether the chosen finals agree, and lists the redo count. Sheet: `docs/art/boards/consistency-stage-vs-bands.png` (a stage plate, then three of its places' bands, rows of 4).
## How a location's stage is found (Core's correction; `fightStageAt`, `lib/combat/fight_ground_rule.dart`)
In order: (a) the scene's forced stage; (b) the stage the place itself names in `geography.json` (a prison or crypt names `hall`; a building is **not** a hall unless it names one); (c) `cityRoad` when the place's **settlement** (its nearest `location`-level ancestor) is a city, town or district; villages and sites do not count; (d) the biome's pattern (`fightStageOf`); (e) open country: `fields`, with a fixed pick by place id among fields, woods, ford and burntFields (`poolStageOf`). A ship is `shipDeck`; a land crossing takes its route's terrain. I re-resolved the 74 locations with exactly this order (`/tmp`-free: the script is in this PR's history); my earlier reading (building = hall) was wrong and is replaced.

## What the check finds
The stage plates are one per stage, but a stage is used by many places of different regions (38 halls, 20 city roads). A single plate cannot match them all.
