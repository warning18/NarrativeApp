# The ink kit (Art, 26 November 2026): sheet first, nothing handed to UX until the user approves

**Generations: 18** (12 first pass: 6 pieces x 2 seeds; 6 retries for the chip, tab and bar; cap 28), text only, small pixflux calls with the five tones plus red forced (`art/pixellab-inkkit/gen.py`), then snapped again in code. Balance 1034 -> 1016.
What PixelLab drew and I kept: the **panel** (a cream card with a thick black border and four dark corner marks), the **button** (a flat red paper rectangle, a lighter top edge), the **bar frame** (a dark navy rounded rectangle, the red fill is code), the **seal** (a round red wax disc with two rings). The **pressed** and **disabled** states, the **chip** and the **tab** are made **in code from those pieces**: PixelLab's own chips came out as two-tone toggles and its tabs as an envelope and folders (6 generations spent on them), so the chip is the panel's border at chip size and the tab is the panel with its bottom border cut off, which keeps the whole kit one family. Pressed = one pixel lower; disabled = the red and the cream turned paper shadow (C9C2A6).
Files `art/pixellab-inkkit/final/<piece>.png` and `nine_slice.json` (the size of each piece and its 9-slice margins left, top, right, bottom): panel (48 x 48, margins 10), button (48 x 24, 8/7), button_pressed, button_disabled, chip (48 x 20, 8), tab (48 x 28, 10/10/10/0: no bottom margin), bar (64 x 16, 5/4), seal (32 x 32, no slice), seal_pressed, seal_disabled. `art/pixellab-inkkit/build.py` rebuilds everything (piece picks, states, 9-slice helper `nine()`).
Sheet `docs/art/boards/inkkit-pieces.png` (each piece at 3x and 1x); mock `docs/art/boards/inkkit-mock.png` (a Tower screen at 3x: three tabs, a panel with two chips and a bar with its red fill, the red TAKE button, the pressed and the locked buttons, three seals), text drawn by code.
Weak spots: the panel's border is thin and a little speckled at its edges (the corner marks read as rivets); the tab and the chip are plainer than the panel. The text in the mock is a default font; the game's own fonts and layout stay code.
