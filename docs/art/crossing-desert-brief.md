**How to draw them:** per layer, offset = `(scroll * speed / unit) mod tileWidth` in scene pixels, two `drawImageRect` draws side by side (FilterQuality.none) to wrap. Speeds are the ones the board was mocked at (today's kit: 0.15, 0.4 and 0.8).
**Under the layers**, in code, rows 52 to 71 are filled with the sand colour **E8AE6D** (the sheet's ground fill) before the mid and near layers, so no sky shows between them.
**Stays code:** the hero (feet at row 66, x 96) and the party, the **sun** (a cream disc, radius 5, centre x = 0.72 of the width, row 30: the sky leaves room for it), the **goal silhouette** (driven by progress; move it to a small code layer over `far.png`), the **path** (rows 64 to 71, speed 1.0; the board's path is an approximation), the weather particles, the encounter figures, and the **dusk wash and stars** (`kit_hour.dart`: the wash is laid over everything, so these layers are tinted like the code ones; stars are drawn behind the layers after the sky). The top 12 rows (under the HUD) are plain violet.
**Honest notes:** the repeat of the mirrored `far` and `near` is visible once per tile on close look; a repair without mirroring is 2 generations (one inpaint a layer), not spent unless the user asks after seeing it in the game. The sky is flatter than the code sky (four flat bands, a dither only on the band edges), as asked.
