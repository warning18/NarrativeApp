# Wave 5b: the 7 foe characters (Art, 25 November 2026)

Full chibi characters for the 7 foes that had none, made in pro mode with `style_character_id` = Starter_basic_hero (the user's rule), 48 px, side view, 8 directions, the (lighter) extended palette forced: `art/pixellab-chars7/gen.py`. Raw rotations in `art/pixellab-chars7/raw/<id>/Idle/rotations/`, kept ones in `art/pixellab-chars7/final/<id>/{west,east,south}.png`. Cost 7 x 10 = **70 generations**, no retries. Character ids in `art/pixellab-chars7/ids.json`.
Characters: horn_taker (a masked exorcist in a dark robe with a curved horn), frost_kept_giant (a pale blue bearded giant with a stone slab on his back), smuggler_captain (a tricorn captain with a lantern), strand_colossus (a hulking dome of keels and teal glass; it reads as a back view), void_wisp (a white flame figure), void_stalker (a tall black plague-mask figure in tatters), void_manifestation (a smoky violet figure with small skulls).
Heads (0 gens): the top 36% of the west frame, `art/pixellab-heads-crops/final/<id>_west.png` and `handoff/<id>.png` (native pixels, same method as the 11 earlier crops). Sheet `docs/art/boards/chars7-sheet.png` (west sprite, head crop beside today's head blob, the wave 4 icon).
The wave 4 icons of these 7 were made from text and differ from the characters (void_wisp, void_stalker, strand_colossus most). I did **not** redraw them from the characters yet: the user's darker direction may redo the foes in a darker look, so I wait for that decision (7 generations, 9 max).
