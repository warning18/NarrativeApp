# Banners in the new style: pilot of 4 (14 generations; nothing staged for UX, nothing under assets/)
Script `art/pixellab-banners-new/gen.py`, sheet builder `art/pixellab-banners-new/sheet.py`, sheet `docs/art/boards/banners-new-style-pilot.png` (3x and game size, dark Tower and parchment; own colours | Apollo + 2 brights | muted palette). Raw in `art/pixellab-banners-new/raw/`, picks in `art/pixellab-banners-new/staged/`.
Method: `create_image_pixflux` img2img from today's `clan_<id>.png` (64x128, no_background), outline single color black, medium shading, medium detail; prompt = "pixel art hanging clan war standard, chunky dark medieval fantasy game style, 1 px black outline, flat colours with a lit top-left edge, hand-stitched look, " + the clan's cloth/emblem/accent in words + direction:
- (a) faithful redraw: "same layout and emblem, brass rod with two gold finials, zig-zag hem, no text"; strength 100 (the first try at 200 changed almost nothing, 4 gens), seed 9: picks.
- (b) bolder: "heavy rod and finials of dark old iron with rivets, a torn cloth hem weighted with small iron weights, the emblem looks stitched onto the cloth, no text"; strength 130, seed 7: picks.
Clans: Dominion (blue lock), Oni (orange horned mask), Crows (red anchor), Mire (teal leaf over waves).
Cost: 8 first tries + 4 for the (a) redo at strength 100 + 2 for the Oni (b) retries (seeds 11, 13, strength 170/140; neither bolder) = **14**.
Honest notes: (a) is a real redraw (a full oni face, a veined leaf, a roped anchor) and keeps each clan recognisable; the Oni mask became a face with fangs, more detailed than today's. (b) gives the heavy iron rod and stitched hem and a quilted cloth but pushes the accent down to a thin line, and Oni (b) lost its mask (rabbit-like ears): weak. Clan accent in today's art is a dark band; in the redraws (a) it is a bolder coloured header.
Palettes: Apollo + 2 brights keeps cream whites and accents readable (cream splits into white and tan: Dominion's lock white, its E tan, a slight blotch); muted flattens the cream to a grey and dims the accents. Clan accents distinguishable? Today's 13 clans, band colour after remap: Apollo 7 distinct of 13, muted 7 of 13 (bands are already dark and close in today's art); by main gem colour: Apollo merges Choir with Compact, muted merges Dominion with Tidekin (blue vs teal). Among the four pilot clans both palettes keep blue, orange, red, green apart; muted makes Dominion blue and Mire green close (both dark teal-blue).
