# Proposal: backgrounds for the character choice, the life-choices header and the expedition screens (no generation before the user's OK)
Sizes from UX-2 (read in code): all screens are 360 or 412 wide, about 780 high.
| screen | what it takes today | what I would draw | generations |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Life choices** (origin_stories_screen) | a 412 x 210 header with the memory's title over its bottom third, faded into the page colour; today a code-drawn scene | one painted **240x100 band per memory place** (scaled 1.72 to 412x172) in the story-scene style: 6 base places (ages 6 sparrow rope-walk in rain, 8, 10 beggar tavern in snow, 12, 14 Old Hesk's house, 16 the parents' house on a winter night); the race (8) and calling (12) variants as a second step | **pilot 2 bands (6 and 16) x 2 seeds = 4**; the other 4 bands x 2 seeds = 8; optional 10 race and calling variants x 1 seed = 10 |
| **Character choice** (race_profession_screen) | plain page colour, a scrolling list of cards and a summary card pinned at the bottom | two **full-bleed plates 180x320** (like the Tower plates, laid under the page colour at about 58%, no layout change): the race step (an empty long table with five different chairs and candles in a road-house) and the calling step (an armoury and study wall: racks, a bow, daggers, a holy symbol, books); no figures | 2 plates x 2 seeds = **4** |
| **Expedition** (expedition_screen) | plain page colour, a goal line, then the cards | one **header band per zone** (20 zones in zones.json), 240x72 scaled 1.72 to 412x124, in the biome and region of the zone, above the goal line | **pilot 3 zones x 2 seeds = 6** (three different biomes); the other 17 zones x 1 seed = 17 |
The Journey's place plan keeps its code biome backdrop (a plate there would have to stay at about 25% under the plan): not proposed. The camp screen and the detour scenes: not read yet.
**Recommendation, in order:** (1) life choices, pilot 4 generations (the header slot already exists, the text is the heart of the game, the code scene reads flat and repeats the same house); (2) character choice, 4 generations (first thing a new player sees); (3) expeditions, pilot 6, then the other 17. Everything is picture first, the banners' and bands' palette (muted world palette, not Apollo), nothing wired; UX-2 wires with no layout change. All three together: pilots 14 generations; complete set 12 + 4 + 23 = 39 (plus 10 optional).
