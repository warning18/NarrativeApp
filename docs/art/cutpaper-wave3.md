## 4. Icons: why they do not match the ink design, and a proposal (8 generations)
Sheet `docs/art/boards/icons-ink-review.png` (today 16 px | the cut-paper test 32 px | the ink-and-spot test 32 px | the Tower's ink look on the right).
Why today's icons and the cut-paper tests do not match the Tower: (1) today's icons are saturated multi-colour fills with soft shading (a green bottle, a blue-grey metal, gold), and the Tower uses paper white, black ink and one deep red; (2) the cut-paper icons are heavy dark bodies with a bright accent hue, which reads as blobs on the paper-white screens, and they bring five different accent hues (green, blue, red, orange, violet, yellow), where the Tower has one; (3) at 16 px there is no room for ink line work.
**Proposed style: ink icon at 32 px**: a thin black ink outline with a little hatching, paper-white fills, and **one spot colour that carries meaning, deep red by default (blood, weapons, power), and a second hue only where the object needs it (green for a cure, blue for ice or water)**. In the test (third row) the potion, dagger, sword, helm and coin read as ink drawings on paper, the boots drew an orange fill over the whole boot and should be redone as an outline with a buckle only; the helm's violet plume should be deep red under the rule. Cost for the whole set: 220 icons at 32 px, one generation each, about 290 with retries, in waves of about 70 (each needing the user's OK); the 157 skill icons stay Apollo. The test files: `art/pixellab-cutpaper/hi/final/inkicon32_*.png`.

Note (the Manager's message reached me after this batch): I had redone all six including light; per the instruction the hand-off keeps the earlier `light_v2` for `skill_light.png` (the v3 light remains in `art/pixellab-cutpaper/tarot/final/light_v3.png`). The redo cost 8 generations (6 + 2 retries), inside the max of 8. The ink-spot icons (`inkicon32_*`, paper ground, black ink outline, one spot colour) are shown next to the cut-paper 32 px set on `docs/art/boards/icons-ink-review.png` (row 2 cut paper, row 3 ink).
