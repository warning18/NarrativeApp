**Icons:** every rarity still reads apart: the shape and frame (diamond, shield, ring, gold square) stay and no icon lost more than 9% of its pixel-to-pixel contrast. The largest merges (one colour fewer, hand check not needed): stone_resolve, cleric_candle_vigil, warrior_iron_stance, dwarf_stoneskin, bulwark_stance, brace. No hand fix made on icons.
**Effects:** hand fix on `void_rift`: nearest colour made its violets grey-pink, so it walks Apollo's purples by brightness (Apollo has no true violet; it is magenta-violet 7A367B / A23E8C / C65197). The others are plain remaps; flame keeps its yellow-white heart through FFF6E0, lightning and heal keep a cream core.

**Tints (for UX-2, `lib/combat/skill_vfx.dart` `_elementPalettes`):** the code re-tints a strip into four tones per element (rank by brightness), so the remapped strips tint almost the same as today's; the tones themselves are not Apollo colours. The `lo` and `deep` tones are mostly within 25 of a palette colour.
