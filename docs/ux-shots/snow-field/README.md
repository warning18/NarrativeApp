# Snow field (v2.10.17)

Normal snowfall on the real Tower fight at 412 wide (`test/ux_snow_field_shots_test.dart`, `UX_SHOTS=<dir>`).

- `snow-field-normal.gif`: 20 s at 2x speed. Drifts build on the top edges of panels and buttons, break off in
  irregular slabs, flakes fall over them; a tap on the foe panel at 14 s knocks its snow off.
- `normal-14s.png`: one frame. `reduced.png`: reduce motion, a static dusting, no flakes.
