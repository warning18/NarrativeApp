# The boss scene (v2.10.13), real floor-20 fights at 412 and 360 wide

Claimant Morrow faces the viewer (Art's south frame) standing whole in the tall
panel; the hero is seen from behind (Art's north frame) in the strip under it,
offset to the right, head and shoulders over the seam, so he never covers the boss.
The hero's plate (name, HP bar) stays on the left of the strip. Names, HP bars, the
Heat chip and the info buttons are where they were.

All 20 bosses and wardens have a south frame (`assets/visuals/boss_south/<id>.png`)
and all 7 heroes a north frame (`assets/visuals/hero_north/<name>.png`), Art's
hand-off, palette PNGs, tinted by the game's ink recolour like the other figures.
A foe with no frame (or a pack) keeps its side view in the 1.5x layout.
