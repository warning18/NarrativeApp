# Three designs of one card (pictures only, no build)
The Mire's gift Focus Mind, uncommon, standing 4/6, at the in-hand size (210 x 346), at rest (top) and lifted/selected (bottom, paper ring). `designs-en.png` (EN), `designs-fr.png` (FR). `test/ux_card_designs_shots_test.dart` draws them (`UX_SHOTS=<dir>`).
- **A · plate**: paper card, the gift's picture on a black plate, name in small caps, the seal the only colour.
- **B · banner**: black card, the clan banner at the left, name on a paper strip, a thin clan line, the clan's motto.
- **C · type**: paper card, the name large, one rule, a round clan stamp, the standing as a tally.
