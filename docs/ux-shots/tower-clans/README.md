# Clans in the Tower, in the tarot style (pictures only, matched to Core's build)

360 and 320 wide, English and French (`<a|b|c>-<name>-<width>-<lang>.png`; `a-offer-rows-320-fr-1.3x` is the rows layout at 320 and 1.3x text). The numbers and words are Core's (`docs/simulation/tower-clan-offers.md`, built on `claude/core-tower-clans-build`): a gift gives +2 to its clan, +1 to allies, −1 to rivals; standing is 0 to 6; the oath comes at 6, one per climb; the strings are Core's and Narrative's. The three skill names are samples. The start pool (Dominion, Marais/Mire, Pénitents) are rivals of each other, so a gift to one costs the other two a step.

- **a. The clan offer.** The Tower's three-card pick, each card a clan's gift: its painted backdrop and banner, the rarity seal, the kind "Gift"/"Don" short (Core's "Gift · Dominion" collides with the banner in the card's corner; the clan is already in the banner and the footer chip "+2 Dominion"). Under the cards, for the card in hand: "Gift · {clan}" line, the motto, Core's ripple line ("Dominion +2 · Mire −1 · Penitents −1") and "Standing 0/6". Buttons: "Not now (+1 mark)" and Accept.
- **b. Standing rising.** (1) The toast when the cards close, Core's lines ("The Dominion thinks more of you. Standing 3 to 5 / Mire Standing 2 to 1 · Penitents Standing 1 to 0"); (2) the six-step bar on the card; (3) a hero-sheet panel with all the clans (not built; a proposal); the rail (dimmed) is too small.
- **c. The oath.** At 6, a banner ("A door opens: the Dominion thinks you worthy of its oath", "Standing 6/6"), then the oath as a fourth, wide card above the floor's three, with Swear / Not now.
