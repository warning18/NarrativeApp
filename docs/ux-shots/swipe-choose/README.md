# Swipe a card up to choose it (v2.10.22)

The Tower's clan offer, 412 wide, real screen.

- `start-nothing-in-hand-412.png`: the middle card is in front, none is in hand, Take is off and a line says "Tap a card, or swipe it up to take it".
- `tap-then-take.gif`: tap the left card (it comes up, the others dim), then Take.
- `swipe-up-choose.gif`: drag the middle card up; past a third of its height (or on a flick) it flies off and is taken at once, the floor moves on.
- `tap-held-412.png`: a card held.

The same applies to the boon and tarot rewards, the rank-ups and the oath. Made by `test/ux_swipe_shots_test.dart` with `UX_SHOTS=<dir>` (one scene per run).
