# Tower coins and the marks shop (pictures only, no build)

`coins-<412|360>-<en|fr>.png` (start with `coins-412-en.png`): one sheet each, in the Tower's ink. Rules and prices are Core's (`docs/proposals/tower-coins.md` on `claude/core-tower-designs`): coins last one climb; marks stay and buy perks and looks after the tree; two perk slots; the boon at 15. `test/ux_tower_coins_shots_test.dart` draws them. The build would be v2.10.1+1079 (after Core's v2.10.0). The card table with reroll and banish is in `../tower-briefs-678/`.

1. **The coin chip** (gold, in-run) and the **Reroll · 3 / Banish · 4** buttons with their rising prices.
2. **The stall** in coins: salve 4, potions 5, the boon 15.
3. **The marks screen as three tabs**: Unlocks (the tree, full: "Marks now buy perks and looks"), Perks (the balance, two worn perks marked "Worn 1" and "Worn 2", owned, affordable and too dear; six of the eight are shown) and Looks (five trims at 10, the Ash trim "earned at Ash, never sold").
4. **The door's two perk slots**, beside the Heat summary.
5. **The end tally**: floors, wardens, bosses, refusals, the Heat bonus, the first-win bonus, the total banked, "Coins left: 9 (lost)" and "Next perk: Fourth card, 12 more marks".

## Guesses
- Perk icons: a letter stands in; Art draws the eight. The perk lines and the trims' names are my placeholders for Narrative.
- The marks use a pale grey square, coins a gold disc, so the two currencies never read alike; the pixel face is not used for numbers (it reads a 5 like an S).
